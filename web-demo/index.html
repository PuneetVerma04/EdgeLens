Steel surface defect detection, entirely in your browser

A YOLO26-nano detector quantised to INT8, running on onnxruntime-web. The image never leaves your machine — there is no backend and no network call after the model downloads. Six NEU-DET classes: crazing, inclusion, patches, pitted surface, rolled-in scale, scratches.

The model is yolo26n_int8.onnx from the EdgeLens pipeline — trained on NEU-DET, exported to ONNX and statically quantised with calibration drawn only from the training split. Its head is end-to-end, so the graph emits final boxes directly and this page implements no non-maximum suppression.

Preprocessing (letterbox to 640, pad 114, RGB, ÷255) is verified against the Python pipeline by web-demo/verify_parity.py. The three casting examples are marked out of domain: they come from an earlier binary classifier and this detector has never seen a casting part, so its boxes on them are meaningless.
